# AI Product Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates AI-generated jewellery creatives for an Indian e-commerce campaign based on the Raksha Bandhan festival.

The objective was to understand how effectively different AI image-generation models can create promotional creatives that are not only visually appealing but also culturally relevant, textually accurate, and commercially usable.

The project compares three AI models across different campaign themes using structured evaluation criteria and human feedback.

---

## Objective

The evaluation aimed to understand:

- Which AI model creates the most commercially usable creative
- How well the models represent the Raksha Bandhan context
- Whether the generated product visuals appear suitable for an e-commerce campaign
- How accurately campaign text is generated
- Whether structured ratings align with actual human preferences

---

## Use Case

**Category:** E-commerce

**Campaign:** Raksha Bandhan Jewellery Promotion

**Product:** Jewellery

The project used a fictional jewellery brand, **SUVARNA JEWELS**, and explored how AI-generated creatives could be used for a realistic social media marketing campaign.

---

## AI Models Evaluated

The following AI image-generation models were compared:

- OpenAI GPT Image
- Google Gemini 3.5 Flash
- Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite

The same campaign requirements were used to maintain a fair comparison between the models.

---

## Campaign Themes

Each model was evaluated across three different campaign themes:

### 1. Royal Indian Festive

A traditional and visually rich festive jewellery advertisement.

### 2. Modern Minimal Luxury

A clean and premium jewellery advertisement with a modern aesthetic.

### 3. Sibling-Gifting Lifestyle

An emotional Raksha Bandhan gifting scene.

This resulted in a total of **9 AI-generated creatives**, with 3 models evaluated across 3 themes.

---

## Evaluation Methodology

A blind human evaluation was conducted using Google Forms with **12 participants**.

The AI model names were not disclosed to participants during the evaluation to reduce potential bias.

Each creative was evaluated on a scale of 1 to 5 based on four criteria:

- **Visual Appeal**
- **Raksha Bandhan Relevance**
- **Text Readability and Accuracy**
- **E-commerce Usability**

Participants were also asked to select their preferred creative within each campaign theme.

This approach combined structured numerical ratings with direct human preferences.

---

## Analysis Approach

The collected responses were analysed using **Microsoft Excel**.

The analysis included:

- Average scores for each creative
- Model-wise performance comparison
- Performance across different evaluation criteria
- Theme-wise comparison
- Direct participant preference analysis

A **Power BI dashboard** was also created to visually present and compare the results.

---

## Key Findings

- No single AI model performed perfectly across all evaluation criteria.
- GPT achieved the highest overall average performance with a score of **4.26 out of 5**.
- Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite achieved an overall average score of **4.10**, followed by Gemini 3.5 Flash with **3.99**.
- GPT received **22 out of 36 total preference votes**.
- GPT performed particularly well in visual appeal, text accuracy, and e-commerce usability.
- Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite showed strong performance in Raksha Bandhan relevance.
- The Sibling-Gifting Lifestyle theme showed the strongest preference pattern, highlighting the importance of emotional storytelling in advertising.
- Structured ratings and direct participant preferences did not always produce the same results, demonstrating the value of combining multiple evaluation methods.

---

## Key Learning

The project demonstrated that an attractive AI-generated image is not automatically suitable for a real-world e-commerce campaign.

For a commercially usable creative, multiple factors need to work together:

**Visual Appeal + Cultural Relevance + Text Accuracy + Product Presentation + E-commerce Usability**

The evaluation also highlighted the importance of combining human feedback with structured analysis when evaluating AI-generated outputs.
